Our Story

An Independent Platform
Built For Exporters

BuyGenix Solutions is a Delhi-based B2B export-import platform founded to bridge the gap between Indian manufacturers and verified international buyers.

We are not a marketplace or aggregator. We are a growth partner — a team of export consultants who work directly with each member, deliver verified leads, and stay with them for the full membership duration.

Every member gets a real, named Relationship Manager available on WhatsApp — because we believe human partnership is what drives real export growth.
