Common Questions

Quick answers to what most people ask us before joining.

How quickly will I receive buyer leads?
Your Relationship Manager reaches out within 24 hours of activation. First leads are typically delivered within 3–5 working days.
Can I upgrade my plan later?
Yes — upgrade from Starter to Growth, Professional or Enterprise at any time. Remaining days are pro-rated against the new plan.
How are buyer leads verified?
Every lead is manually reviewed by our team. We verify the buyer's company, product requirements and contact details before sending.
Do I need an IEC code to join?
No — we help you apply for IEC and other registrations as part of your membership. You can receive leads while your application is in progress.
What product categories do you cover?
We cover 12+ categories including textiles, spices, agricultural products, handicrafts, engineering goods, garments, leather, chemicals and gems.
Is there a refund policy?
Please review our Terms & Conditions for full refund and cancellation details. For specific queries, contact us on WhatsApp before purchasing.
